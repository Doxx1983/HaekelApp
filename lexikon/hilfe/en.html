What the glossary does

Look up stitches, abbreviations and terms from crochet patterns, in English and German. Tap an entry to open it: an explanation, often with the individual steps, and the names in US English, UK English and German.

Search

Enter a name or an abbreviation as written in the pattern, e.g. sc2tog, inc, BLO, sl st, treble or a German one like fM. Upper/lower case, dots and spaces don’t matter. Exact abbreviation matches come first.

For abbreviations that mean different things in US and UK patterns (dc, tr, dtr, dc2tog), a note shows both meanings.

Filters

  • Areas: basic stitches, increases & decreases, where to insert, texture stitches, terms, techniques.
  • Difficulty: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.

Tap again to remove the filter. Filters and search can be combined.

Favourites

The star on the right of an entry saves it. ★ Favourites shows only those, handy for the abbreviations of your current pattern.

US · UK · DE

The table puts the terms from American, British and German patterns side by side. Rows where US and UK use different abbreviations are highlighted.

Rule of thumb: UK counts one step higher. The basic short stitch is “single crochet” (sc) in US and “double crochet” (dc) in UK. If a pattern uses sc, it is American.

Tap the German name to open the entry with its explanation.

Saving

Favourites and filters stay saved in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. The favourites are also part of the backup on the start page; restoring adds missing ones.
