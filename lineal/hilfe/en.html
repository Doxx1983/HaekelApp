What the ruler does

Like a ruler you lay on a paper pattern while crocheting: a strip marks the row you are on, everything above is faded. Next › moves it down one row and counts along. Good for everything the Crochet Helper cannot read: photos, scanned PDFs, crochet charts and grids.

Opening a pattern

  • Open image or PDF: the file goes into the Pattern Collection and opens here. That way the file and your position are kept.
  • From the collection: all images and PDFs in the collection are listed, most recently used first, with the row you were on. In the collection you can also use Open in Ruler.
  • Patterns at the top right takes you back to the list.

Working with the ruler

  • Place it: tap the spot in the pattern where you are, or drag the strip there by its round handle.
  • Set the width: ▁ and ▃ make the strip narrower or wider. Set it so it covers exactly one row of the pattern.
  • Next › moves it down by its own width and counts one row on, ‹ Back goes back one.
  • Correct the row: tap “Row …” at the top and enter the right number.

View

− and + zoom in, e.g. for small grids. The ruler stays in place. Multi-page PDFs are shown one below the other.

Saving

For each pattern the ruler remembers position, width, zoom and row, in this browser on this device (the 50 most recently used).

You can back up everything on the start page under Back up your data: one file with projects, counters, grid, collection and ruler positions. If the last backup was more than 14 days ago, every app reminds you.
