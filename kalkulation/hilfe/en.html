What pricing does

It works out a selling price for a project from working time, materials, extra costs, profit and tax.

The projects are those from the Crochet Helper. If there are none yet, create a project there first.

The calculation is a guide, not tax advice.

Labour

  • The time comes from the Stopwatch, rounded as set there. If the stopwatch is still running, pricing keeps counting along.
  • Enter time: type the time by hand as h:mm (3:45) or in minutes. Time from stopwatch goes back to the measured time.
  • The hourly rate comes from the stopwatch; you can change it here for the calculation. The stopwatch stays as it is.

Materials

  • + Material adds an item.
  • Pieces: quantity × price per piece, e.g. 2 balls × 3.95 €.
  • Grams: grams used × price per 100 g, e.g. 80 g at 4.95 € per 100 g = 3.96 €.
  • Add more items for supplies like stuffing, eyes or labels. × deletes an item.

Extra costs

Fixed amounts like packaging, shipping, stall fee or selling fees, one line per item.

Profit and tax

  • Profit: a percentage added on top of your costs (labour + materials + extra costs). The last value you entered is suggested for new projects.
  • VAT: none, 7 % or 19 % (German rates). Which rate applies to you is a tax question; the calculator only uses the rate you choose. The setting applies to all projects.

Price

  • Round final price to: exact, 10 cents, 50 cents or whole euros; standard to the nearest step or always round up.
  • Below the final price you see what you keep per hour at that price.
  • Save calculation stores the final price and all parts with the date for the project. A new calculation replaces the old one after asking.
  • Share sends the calculation as text (on a computer: to the clipboard).

Saving

Everything is stored in this browser on this device. If a project is deleted in the Crochet Helper, it also disappears from the list here.

You can back up everything on the start page under Back up your data. If the last backup is more than 14 days old, every app reminds you.
