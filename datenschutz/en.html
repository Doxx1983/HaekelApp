In short

  • No account, no cookies, no tracking, no ads.
  • Everything you enter or open in the apps stays on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Only when the pages load does the host see your IP address.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The apps are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, GitHub stores your IP address in server logs for security purposes, together with technical details such as time and requested file.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and reliable delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub decides how long it keeps the logs: GitHub privacy statement.

Storage on your device

To work, the apps store data in your browser's storage (localStorage, IndexedDB) and keep a copy of the app files for offline use (service worker). This is strictly necessary for the feature you use and needs no consent (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

This includes projects and patterns, progress and colours, counters, grids, items in the pattern collection, ruler positions, stopwatch times, price calculations, glossary favourites, your language and the date of your last backup. This data is not transmitted. You delete it in the apps or in your browser settings ("clear site data").

Opening files

Images, PDFs, Word and text files you open are read by the browser on your device. They are not uploaded.

Backup

The backup file is created on your device and saved where you choose. We do not receive a copy.

Sharing and clipboard

When you share, for example, a price calculation, the text goes only on your tap to the app you choose, or to the clipboard.

No third-party services

Fonts and code are part of the app. No analytics tools, font servers or social networks are included.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). Contact info@isadody.de.

Right to object under Art. 21 GDPR

Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). Our competent authority is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de.

Automated decisions

No automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR), takes place.
