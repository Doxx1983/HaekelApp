What the row counter does

The counter looks like a pop-it: each bubble is one row. When you finish a row, you press a bubble. The top left shows which row you are on.

Projects

You can create up to 10 projects, e.g. one per hat or blanket. Each has its own count, its own bubbles and its own goal. There is also the free counter, which belongs to no project.

  • Under Settings → Project, tap a project to switch to it.
  • New project: type a name and tap + Add. It starts at 0. If the name already exists, the app asks whether to overwrite the existing project (reset it to 0).
  • Rename: change the name in the field below the list. Delete removes the project and its count after asking. The free counter cannot be deleted.
  • Below the row number at the top you see which project is running.

The counter’s projects are separate from those in the Crochet Helper.

Counting

  • Tap a bubble: it and all bubbles before it are pressed. The top then shows its row number, which is also printed on each bubble.
  • Tap a pressed bubble: it and all bubbles after it pop back up. The count is then the bubble before it.
  • Row done presses the next free bubble.
  • − 1 takes back the last pressed bubble, even if the board has just flipped.

While the counter is open, the screen stays on (if the browser supports it).

Below “Row” you see e.g. “Board 2 · 3/10”: you are on the second board and 3 of 10 bubbles are pressed.

Full board

When all bubbles are pressed, the board flips over like a real pop-it. All bubbles are up again and counting continues. With 10 bubbles, the second board shows numbers 11 to 20.

Settings

Tap Settings at the top right. Done applies your changes.

  • Bubbles per board: 6 to 30, 10 to start with.
  • Goal: how many rows does your piece have? A bar then shows your progress, and the top says “12 to go” or “Goal reached”. Leave empty if you don’t need a goal.
  • Count: shows the current row. You can set it by hand here, e.g. if you kept crocheting without the app. Leaving it empty changes nothing; entering 0 asks first.
  • Turn the pop sound and vibration on or off (applies to all projects). Vibration only works on devices that support it.
  • Reset to 0 sets the count to 0 after asking. It is only saved with “Done”.

Saving

Count and settings stay saved in this browser on this device, even after closing. They are not available on another device or in a private window.

You can back up everything on the start page under Back up your data: one file with projects, counters, grid and collection. It also shows when you last backed up. If it was more than 14 days ago, every app reminds you. On iPhone, add the page via “Share” → “Add to Home Screen”, otherwise Safari deletes the data after 7 days without use.

If the browser storage is full, the app tells you. Then back up and delete old things.
